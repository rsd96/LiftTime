# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 20, 2024

This Privacy Policy describes how Lift Time ("we," "us," or "our") handles your information.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**Lift Time does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or Health Data from your device.**

Our application provides a rest timer for gym activities. While the app is designed for use during physical exercise, it **does not access, collect, or process any health-related sensors or data** (such as heart rate, steps, or calories) from your device or any fitness platforms (like Google Fit).

## 2. Data Accessed

The application requires the following access to function:
* **Foreground Service:** To maintain the rest timer and screen-state monitoring while you are using the app in the gym.
* **Screen State (Intent.ACTION_SCREEN_ON/OFF):** To automatically trigger the rest timer when you interact with your device.

This data is processed locally on your device in real-time and is **never stored or transmitted** to our servers or any third parties.

## 3. Third-Party Services

If you installed this application from the Google Play Store, Google Play Services may collect certain information. We do not have access to this data. You can review their privacy policy here: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

* **Retention:** Since we do not collect or store any user data on our servers, we do not retain any of your information. Any configuration settings (such as your preferred timer duration) are stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences.
* **Deletion:** You can delete all locally stored app data at any time by:
    1. Clearing the app cache and data in your Android Settings.
    2. Uninstalling the application from your device.
    Once the app is uninstalled, all locally stored settings are permanently removed.

## 5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

## 6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us!
